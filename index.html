EARTHQUAKE

SAFETY PROCEDURES

🏠

In Home

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold on under heavy furniture.
  • Stay away from glass windows and cabinets.
  • Stay in bed and protect your head with a pillow.
🛍️

In Mall

  • Don't rush for the exits; avoid the crowd.
  • Stay clear of glass displays and mirrors.
  • Find cover near structural pillars.
🏢

In Building

  • Never use elevators under any circumstances.
  • Stay inside until the shaking completely stops.
  • Move away from the exterior walls of the building.
🏫

In School

  • Immediately get under your desk or table.
  • Keep away from windows and heavy bookshelves.
  • Wait for official instructions before evacuating.
